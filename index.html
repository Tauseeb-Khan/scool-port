About our school

Scholars’ Group of Institutions offers education from Nursery to Class 8, aligned with the ICSE curriculum. Experienced faculty, technology, moral education and real-world skills shape future-ready learners in Lucknow.

Our early-years programme is play-based and hands-on for children aged 2 to 5, building problem-solving, communication and creativity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which board does Scholars’ Paradise follow?

The school follows the ICSE curriculum in English medium, from Nursery to Class 8.

Which classes are available?

Nursery to Class 8. Branch-wise class availability is confirmed by each campus, so please check the campus page or call the school.

Where are the campuses in Lucknow?

There are six campuses: Vikas Nagar, Gaurabagh, Muazzam Nagar, Kursi, Kalyanpur and Ring Road. Use the campus finder to see the one nearest to your home.

Are admissions open for 2026–27?

Yes. Admissions are open for 2026–27. Fill in the admission enquiry form and the nearest campus will call you back.

Does the school have smart classrooms?

Yes. The school uses LEAD smart classes, digital tools and activity-based learning in its classrooms.

Is the campus safe for young children?

The school describes a safe, hygienic environment with CCTV surveillance and a low student-teacher ratio.

Does the school offer Hifz along with regular studies?

Yes. Scholars’ Hifz Academy offers Quran memorisation with Tajweed alongside regular ICSE schooling.

What are the school fees?

Fees depend on the class and campus. Please submit an enquiry or call +91-6393477299 and the campus will share the current fee structure.
